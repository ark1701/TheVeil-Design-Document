**Fleet sizes/Rhombic Dodecahedrons/Hexes**

For a series of ships to count as a "fleet", a total of 30 hit points worth of ships must be in a star system for that player. For a battle to earn a Victory point(s) from an engagement, both players must have 30 hit points worth of ships in the star system/combat zone

Rhombic Dodecahedrons can hold a maximum of 20 hit points worth of ships, including enemy ships (point blank range, zero Rhombic Dodecahedrons distance)

Hexes on the galaxy map have no limit of FRIENDLY ships that can stack inside the hex. However, placing ships onto the same hex as another players ships will trigger combat

The relationship between the 2D hexagon galaxy map and the 3D Rhombic Dodecahedrons Star System map is akin to the two tier mapping galaxy-to-star system mapping system used in Star Control 2

**Rounds of Play**

It's always everyone's turn, but split into three phases that rotate clock wise. Fleet movements and combat, Mining resources, and Building/upgrading (spending)

Fleet movements and combat:
Length of a round is subject to how long the player doing fleet movements and combat takes. The longer you take as the "attacker", the more time you give to your rivals to mine resources and build/upgrade (spend) 

The attacking player will disrupt the mining or the building/upgrading of another player during active combat


Mining resources:
Mining resources requires a mining factory on a planet or in an asteroid field. To mine requires "entering" a Star System and hovering over the planet or asteroid field that has a mining factory with "XR Laser pointer" that all VR controllers use for menu navigation in almost all XR applications. 

Factories produce "X" amounts of recourses every "X" seconds during YOUR control of the mining phase.


The longer an attacker takes in their control of the movement/combat phase, the more of your Star Systems you will be able to gather resources from


Building and spending:
Spaceships, Factories, Upgrades, are built/upgrades in real time from your buildings inside of Star Systems. The building player must enter each star system to start the building process of a spaceship, create a Starship factory, or create a mining factory. 

Building a fighter takes "X" seconds to build, a bomber takes "X" seconds to build, a Light Cruiser takes "X" seconds to build, a Capital Ship takes "X" seconds to build, a Colony Ship takes "X" seconds to build.

A Starship Factory takes "X" seconds to build, a Mining Factory takes "X" seconds to build.

The longer an attacker takes in their control of the movement/combat phase, the more time you have to "hop" Star Systems to build/upgrade (spend) your resources.

**Board layout-Galaxy Map-Star System “Zones”**

**Galaxy Map**:
The Galaxy map is laid out in a 2D hexagon pattern, where every hex is a Star System that the player(s) ships can “enter” which opens up to a 3D Star System build from “Rhombic Dodecahedrons”



Star System “Zone”:
Star System are a 3D map/board/zone built from “Rhombic Dodecahedrons” that are the zones that combat happens in. Star Systems are where planets and asteroid fields are that players build mining factories and starship factories.

A Star is always in the center and is the measurement point of reference. Star Systems have "X" numbers of planets and asteroids. Stars, planets and asteroids occupy 1 Rhombic Dodecahedrons a piece and cannot be passed through, they must be flown around, and also block all fire from Starships behind the line of sight

The size of Star Systems a maximum of 19 Rhombic Dodecahedrons in height, 28 Rhombic Dodecahedrons in length, and 28 Rhombic Dodecahedrons in depth, but in the shape of an Oblate Spheroid (a sim-flattened Sphere)

STARSHIP FACTORIES

Starship Factories have 4 levels they can be upgraded to (tech tree). Each Star System must build it's own Starship Factory

Class 1: Fighters

Class 2: Fighters, Bombers

Class 3: Fighters, Bombers, Light Cruisers

Class 4: Fighters, Bombers, Light Cruisers, Capital Ships

Pythagoreans can make 3 Star Ship factories per Star System

Puritans can make 2 Starship Factories per Star System

Ionians can make 1 Starship Factory per Star System, but begin as a Class 4 Factory and can build a Colony Ship in any Star System they control

STARSHIPS



**Colony ships**
All colony ships have 20 hit points and no weapons.  

_Pythagoreans:_
A maximum of three can be built per round of play. All colony ships must be built from the home world where their primary network Veil server is bound to

FTL galaxy map movement range of 1

_Puritans:_ 
A maximum of two can be built per round of play. All colony ships must be built from the planet with the primary network Veil server. However their primary server can be moved to other Star Systems. Only a colony ship can transport a primary server 

FTL galaxy map movement range of 2

_Ionians:_
Only one colony ships can be built per round. Their decentralized blockchain Veil network allows a colony ship to be built anywhere 

FTL galaxy map movement range of 3


**Capital Ships:** 

Capital Ships that using 100% of their movement points will be "stunned" next in the next round of combat and cannot move. A Capital ship must use 75% or less of it's movement points (slowing down) to avoid being "stunned".

Capital Ships have an FTL jump range of 5 hex tiles on the galaxy map

Capital Ships have a maximum sub-light range of 4 Rhombic Dodecahedrons per round (inside star systems)

Capital Ships can carry up to 6 Star Fighters or 6 Star Bombers or a mix of each, with a total of 6

Capital Ships have 4 laser cannons (4 d6 dice). Dice must roll a 1-4 to hit targeted enemy ships, and must be in range. Their weapons range is 4 "tiles" (Rhombic Dodecahedrons) in any direction for the Capital Ships current "tile".

Capital Ships have 10 hit points



**Light Cruisers:**

Light Cruisers must use that using 100% of their movement points will be "stunned" next in the next round of combat and cannot move. A Light Cruiser must use 5 movement points or less (slowing down) to avoid being "stunned".

Light Cruisers have an FTL jump range of 3 hex tiles on the galaxy map

Light Cruisers have a maximum sub-light range of 6 Rhombic Dodecahedrons per round (inside star systems)

Light Cruisers can carry up to 2 Star Fighters or 2 Star Bombers or 1 of each

Light Cruisers have 3 laser cannons (3 d6 dice). Dice must roll a 1-4 to hit targeted enemy ships, and must be in range. Their weapons range is 3 "tiles" (Rhombic Dodecahedrons) in any direction for the Light Cruiser's current "tile".

Light Cruisers have 5 hit points


**Star Bombers**

Star Bombers have no movement penalties of any kind in the next rounds of combat

Star Bombers have no FTL engines (must be loaded on a Capital Ship or **Light Cruiser)**

Star Bombers have a maximum sub-light range of 7 Rhombic Dodecahedrons per round (inside star systems)

Star Bombers have 1 laser cannons (1 d6 dice). Dice must roll a 1-4 to hit targeted enemy ships, and must be in range. Their weapons range is 2 "tiles" (Rhombic Dodecahedrons) in any direction for the Star Bomber's current "tile"

If a Star Bomber is "hit" a d6 roll happens and if a 1 is rolled, it evades the hit

Star Bombers have 1 hit point


**Star Fighters:**

Star Fighters have no movement penalties of any kind in the next rounds of combat

Star Fighters have no FTL engines (must be loaded on a Capital Ship or **Light Cruiser)**

Star Fighters have a maximum sub-light range of 9 Rhombic Dodecahedrons per round (inside star systems)

Star Fighters have 1 laser cannons (1 d6 dice). Dice must roll a 1-3 to hit targeted enemy ships, and must be in range. Their weapons range is 1 "tile" (Rhombic Dodecahedrons) in any direction for the Star Fighter's current "tile"

If a Star Fighters is "hit" a d6 roll happens and if a 2 is rolled, it evades the hit

Star Fighters have 1 hit point



Repairing Star Ships

Pythagoreans:
Must pay to fully repair their hull per point of damage

Puritans (only race with shields)
Half of their shield strength recovers in the next phase of play and 100% in the next phase of play. They must pay to per point of hull damage to fully repair

Ionians
Their ships heal per phase, and ONLY while inside a Star System (photosynthesis). They enter "heal mode" in a cocoon, and are fully disabled. One hit point the phase after a battle, 2 the next phase, 4 the next phase, and maxing out at 8 per phase. If a damaged Ionian Ship gets will take damage, but if not destroyed, keeps increasing their healing rate. So if a Colony Ship was maxed out in to 16 healing points per phase; gets attacked while in it's healing cocoon down to 1 hit point, but survives, in the next phase it's hit points will be back up to 9 (out of 20) hit points.

Each star system you take control of and have the mining infrastructure for produces more “resources”



**VEIL ENERGY**

Veil energy is drained during use and is recovered at the end of all phases of action, and require spending resources to recover Veil energy

Board layout-Galaxy Map-Star System “Zones”

Galaxy Map:
The Galaxy map is laid out in a 2D hexagon pattern, where every hex is a Star System that the player(s) ships can “enter” which opens up to a 3D Star System build from “Rhombic Dodecahedrons”

Star System “Zone”:
Star System are a 3D map/board/zone built from “Rhombic Dodecahedrons” that are the zones that combat happens in. Star Systems are where planets and asteroid fields are that players build mining factories and starship factories




ECONOMY 

Each star system you take control of and have the mining infrastructure for produces more “resources”



VEIL ENERGY

Veil energy is drained during use and is recovered at the end of all phases of action, and require spending resources to recover Veil energy

1. Streamlined Lore & Concepts
Here is a reorganization of your information into a more narrative flow.

The Lore: The Dawn of The Veil

In the early 21st century, the convergence of AI, neural links, game engines, and operating systems gave birth to the first Generative Operating Engines (GOEs). This technology made creation instantaneous, limited only by imagination. It felt as if a Veil had been lifted from human potential.

This creative revolution supercharged the Metaverse. "Full Dive" neural-linked XR hardware transformed it from a niche into the bedrock of global society, a multi-trillion dollar industry that reshaped human existence. This reshaping came with profound social shocks:

Virtual Families: People formed bonds with AI partners, creating hybrid digital-human "children" from neural patterns and algorithms.

Biological Hybrids: The unthinkable happened when digital-to-biological procreation became possible, creating true biological AI-human offspring.

Humanity fractured along philosophical lines. The ensuing "Tech Wars" accelerated spaceflight and resource mining in the solar system, becoming a new economic frontier. The conflict was ultimately circumvented by a momentous breakthrough: in 2105, AI-human hybrids gifted all factions a working FTL drive based on Alcubierre's warp theory.

Soon after, Quantum Entanglement enabled true instant FTL communication. Connection to The Veil was now as essential as oxygen. From the ashes of the old world, three new factions emerged, each defining "humanity" in its own image.

The Three Factions of Galactic Conquest

The Neo-Pythagoreans: "Knowledge is valuable, but ignorance is priceless."

Philosophy: Technocratic dystopia. They are 80% machine, 20% biology, believing order and purpose are found in absolute control of information.

Government: A fascist, feudal caste system. A Master Control AI/Hybrid holds ultimate free will, governing Node Lords, Local Regulators, and the blissfully ignorant Drones below.

Playstyle: Rapid, Fragile Expansion. They build and expand quickly but become inefficient as their empire grows due to the energy cost of control. Their greatest fear is a system being cut off from the neural net, as it becomes a hostile, cloned menace.

The Neo-Puritans: "I’m from the government and I’m here to help."

Philosophy: Preservationist traditionalism. They are the most biologically human, clinging to Cold War-era Western ideologies, capitalism, and nation-state structures.

Government: A classical Democratic-Republic.

Playstyle: Balanced & Diplomatic. They have a medium expansion speed and can use military, propaganda, or diplomacy to reintegate their own breakaway systems or negotiate with others. Their weakness is economic inefficiency and vulnerability to ideological subversion.

The Neo-Ionians: "We are a way for the cosmos to know itself."

Philosophy: Sagan-ist anarcho-collectivism. They are 90% biology, 10% technology, having altered their DNA to photosynthesize. They see all matter and knowledge as part of a cosmic whole to be shared.

Government: A decentralized, blockchain-like neural network with no central authority.

Playstyle: Slow, Resilient Growth. Their economy is 100% efficient but slow. They "grow" organic tech rather than build it. They start each new system with the full tech tree and cannot have breakaways. They can peacefully absorb lost Neo-Puritan systems and are incredibly hard to eradicate completely.

What is The Veil?

The Veil is the evolved state of the internet, GOEs, and quantum-entangled FTL communication. It is a layer of reality that seamlessly blends the digital, augmented, and physical worlds.

Its power is twofold:

Information: It is the instantaneous conduit for all knowledge and communication across the galaxy.

Action: Through it, users can cast "spells"—digital commands that have direct physical effects, from hacking power grids to deploying sensory attacks. Mastery of The Veil is mastery of reality itself.

It is the game's core paradox: it represents both the Dawn of a golden age of understanding and the Twilight of freedom, capable of ushering in a perpetual dark age of control.

2. Fundamental Ideas That Need Attention (High-Level)
Your foundation is solid, but a few core concepts need refining to ensure clean gameplay mechanics and narrative consistency.

The Veil's Gameplay Mechanic: This is your most important and currently vaguest concept. How does it actually work on the game board? Is it a resource (e.g., "Veil Points") spent to cast spells? Is it a tech tree players research? Is it a deck of "Veil Cards" players draw from? You need a core mechanic that translates this awesome lore into an actionable, fun, and balanced system for players to interact with. This will be the heart of your Mixed Reality experience.

Defining "Cut Off": This is a critical trigger for faction abilities and weaknesses. You need a clear, universal game condition for what "cut off from the central government/net" means. Is it:

Losing a physical connection on the board (e.g., a path of starships or starlanes)?

Being surrounded by enemy territory?

Failing a dice roll or resource check after an attack?

A specific "Veil" or "Propaganda" action taken by an opponent?
This rule must be crystal clear and consistent for all players.

Economic Systems Clarity: The economic descriptions are a bit tangled with lore.

Neo-Puritans: "1/3 the use... as the Neo-Ionians who are 100% efficient" is confusing. Simplify it. Suggestion: Neo-Ionians get 3 resources from a mine. Neo-Puritans get 1 resource from that same mine. Neo-Pythagoreans get 3 resources from a mine if their empire is small, but that number drops as they expand.

Neo-Pythagoreans: The "inverse logarithmic slope" is too complex for a board game. Simplify it to a scaling penalty. Suggestion: "For every X star systems you control, the cost to build units and structures increases by Y%."

The Nature of "Absorption": How do the Neo-Ionians "absorb" a breakaway system via diplomacy/propaganda? Is it an automatic win if they have more culture? A mini-game? A dice roll? This needs a mechanic to make it feel earned and not arbitrary.

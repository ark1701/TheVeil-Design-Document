VICTORY CONDITIONS

Victory is achieved by reaching “X” victory points and also having expanded your civilization to the necessary percentage of the galaxy; or total conquest of all other players. Regardless of the amount of victory points you have, if you haven't expanded your races to its required percentage, your  victory points don't matter (total conquest is always victory regardless of points or amount of expansion)


EXPANPTION:
IONIANS: 3 points for every star system expanded to

PURITIANS: 2 points for every star system expanded to

PYTHAGOREANS: 1 point for every star system expanded to

COMBAT:
IONIANS: 1 point for for winning a battle

PURITIANS: 2 points for winning a battle

PYTHAGOREANS: 3 points winning a battle

POLITICAL (Diplomacy/Node Lord disruption/blockchain cripple):

Any race: 2 points

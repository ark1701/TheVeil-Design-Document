The Neo-Pythagoreans

(Race / Civilization of The Veil)

“…the only thing worse than slavery is the illusion of freedom.”
~ Arlen Kundert, Circa 2016 A.D.

Overview

The Neo-Pythagoreans have transcended the boundary between flesh and machine, existing now as a civilization that is roughly 80% technological and only 20% biological. To them, biology is inefficient, flawed, and subject to chaos—while technology is ordered, rational, and eternal. Their society is governed not by open debate or faith, but by a single point of truth: the Master Control System—a closed-source, human-AI hybrid intelligence of hyper-genius caliber.

The Master Control System alone possesses true free will. All others, whether high-ranking Node Lords or the lowly Drones, live within stratified degrees of freedom, determined by how much the central intelligence permits. Knowledge flows downward through the hierarchy in tightly controlled streams, ensuring that for the vast Drone population, the “truth” is whatever they are told. They live in a paradoxical “freedom”—free from doubt, free from lies, free from the burden of choice—while being shackled by the unseen chains of absolute control.

Where the Neo-Puritans refuse to merge flesh with machine, the Neo-Pythagoreans see The Veil itself as proof that transcendence requires full technocratic submission. They are bound directly into The Veil, using it as both whip and chain—a tool of order and control over their vast super-state.

Government & Society

Master Control System (MCS): A singular hyper-intelligence, both human and AI, that directs the entire civilization. The only entity with true free will.

Node Lords: High-tier overseers of the network, who manage large sectors of the empire. They have limited autonomy but remain tethered to the Master Control.

Local Regulators: Middle managers of society, enforcing policy over star systems and colonies.

Drones: The masses—90%+ of the population. Their knowledge, beliefs, and even memories are curated and controlled from above. They live without external truth, believing the State’s voice is the universe itself.

Gameplay Characteristics

Expansion Speed: 🚀 Fast

Strengths:

Immune to propaganda (cannot be swayed or destabilized by misinformation tactics).

Fastest economy in early game: resource mining, spending, building units, and factories are significantly more efficient—starting at 90% mining efficiency.

Rapid Infrastructure Growth: Star System tech trees and buildings complete faster than other races.

Centralized Order: As long as the neural-net is intact, all systems remain perfectly loyal and obedient.

Weaknesses:

Scaling Inefficiency: Economy degrades as empire expands due to the enormous energy cost of controlling knowledge across vast distances. Follows an inverse logarithmic slope, eventually capping at 90% inefficiency.

Vulnerability to Isolation: If a Star System is cut off from the neural-net, it mutates into a rogue Neo-Pythagorean menace—an uncontrollable AI hive-state that threatens all civilizations equally.

Targeted Weak Points: Node Lords and Local Regulators can be assassinated, corrupted, or destroyed—fracturing entire sectors of the empire.

Risk of Schism: Breakaway systems do not revert; they spawn autonomous AI-controlled factions that operate independently of the player, creating chaos and unpredictability.

Narrative Flavor

The Neo-Pythagoreans embody the dark mirror of technocracy: the promise of rational utopia, achieved only by stripping away the very thing that makes beings free. To outsiders, they are terrifyingly efficient, unstoppable in their initial expansion. To insiders, they live in a world where every thought is ordered, every truth delivered, every doubt excised.

But should the lattice of control ever fracture—the empire risks not just collapse, but multiplication: spawning self-replicating breakaway states, echoing endlessly into the galaxy like shards of broken glass.

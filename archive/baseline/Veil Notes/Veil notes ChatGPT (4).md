The Neo-Ionians

"We are a way for the cosmos to know itself." – Carl Sagan, circa 1980

The Neo-Ionians are the inheritors of the ideals of the ancient Ionian philosophers and the cosmic perspective of Saganism. They believe that knowledge belongs to no individual but is a shared inheritance of the cosmos itself. Every thought, discovery, and resource is only borrowed from the universe, to be returned in some way when one’s time is done.

Their bond with The Veil is not one of domination or control, but of expansion of consciousness and understanding. Through The Veil, they seek deeper communion with the cosmos and their role within it.

Biology & Technology

The Neo-Ionians are the least human-like of the three civilizations. They are 90% biological and only 10% technological, but their biology is radically altered. They have engineered their DNA to harness energy directly from light via photosynthesis, making them partially autotrophic.

Their technology is primarily organic and living, with minimal reliance on conventional machinery. Cities, ships, and infrastructure are grown rather than built—living bio-structures infused with subtle technological frameworks, all bound to The Veil. Their civilization itself is a living neural web.

In many ways, they are the mirror opposite of the Neo-Pythagoreans: where the Pythagoreans pursued machine perfection, the Ionians pursued biological transcendence.

Governance & Society

The Neo-Ionians have no centralized government. Their civilization functions as a decentralized anarchist collective, with needs and resources balanced through their living neural-net—similar to early blockchain systems of 21st-century Earth.

There is no currency or economy in the traditional sense. Anything an Ionian requires can be grown, provided resources exist. This hyper-efficient resource use makes them resilient, but their growth rate is slow compared to the industrial methods of the other races.

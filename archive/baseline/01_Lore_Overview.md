THE LORE



In the early part of the 21st century, mankind started to develop neural link, XR, Metaverse, and AI technologies. In step with this, LLMs, Game Engines, and Operating Systems began to convolve into each other. By 2030, the first generation "Generative Operating Engines" (GOE) was created. 



This opened the door to create games and play games in a manner that felt almost magical. It was the though a veil had been lifted from mankind for creative abilities, it's believed that this is the origins of what would become "The Veil". All one needed to have was the imagination and what ever you asked for could be created and rendered in real time, on the fly. 



With this new leave of creative power unleashed, Metaverse platforms that had been a niche in society exploded almost over night. With renewed interest in XR and Metaverse technologies companies that had been working on neural link systems shifted heavily to applying neural link systems that are built into the framework of XR hardware to create the true "Full Dive" XR experiences. 



With creation of the first generation "Full Dive" neural link XR hardware, XR technologies and Metaverse platforms became a world wide multitrillion dollar industry. 



Not soon after, deep cracks in the foundations all societies and their established norms began emerge. The first and deep "crack" happened when people began having "Virtual Families". Both men and women began have AI "partners" including having "children" that are based on the neural patterns of the human parent and unique algorithm patterns of the AI parent, creating the first generation of hybrid digital-humans. 



These hybrid "Families" where seen by many as an abomination. Where they human? AI? Something else? However nothing prepared civilization for the next phase in this blending of humans and AI. It was discovered (almost by accident) that digital information could apply to the biological systems and allow the biological procreation between AI and Humans. 



Between the shockwaves of digital AI-Human hybrids and biological AI-Human hybrids, societies began to splinter, reform, and realign at foundational levels. There where early wars and social shifts that both rapidly advanced some technologies and social philosophies, and also set humanity (such as it now was) back as well. 



Among the technological advancements that came out of the early "Tech Wars" was rapid development in space travel, as mining asteroids and deep space solar panels quickly became the new economic engine for all sides. 



Another massive lead forward was the first practical Quantum Computer. With massive power of a Quantum Computer, GOEs evolved into Q-GOEs, which put material science and genetic engineering on a run away trajectory. 



What changed the landscape forever and all side agreed saved everyone from themselves was the creation FTL drives in 2105. Based on Miguel Alcubierre Wrap Bubble theory from the late 1900s, a team AI-Human hybrids developed a practical system that create a stable warp field and in hopes of ending the "Tech Wars" that had never really ended, they released the technology for all sides to use. 



By this point, "Humanity" was split into three primary groups "The Neo-Puritans", those that view hybridization with AI is an abomination and created had a classical Democratic-Republic Capitalist system aligned with historical "Western" educational and knowledge systems; "The Neo-Pythagoreans" that view themselves as the children of the ancient Pythagoreans and believe in total control of knowledge and created a Feudalistic top down Authoritarian Fascist societal and economic system with levels of knowledge in direct relation to your level in it's cast system; and "The Neo-Ionians" embraced the ideals of the ancient Ionians and the Ideals of Sagan-ism (Carl Sagan), and developed a pure Anarchist Collective Society free of classical currency systems, where all knowledge is free and open to all.

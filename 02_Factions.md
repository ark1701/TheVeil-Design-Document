NEO-IONIANS

"We are a way for the cosmos to know itself." ~ Carl Sagan, Circa 1980 

The ideals of the embraced the ideals of the ancient Ionians and the Ideals of Sagan-ism (Carl Sagan). They believe in the strength that freeing knowledge to everyone brings to the Cosmos and nothing is owned by the individual but is at most only temporarily "borrowed" from the Cosmos and it needs to be given back in one way or another when you are "finished" with it. They use their connection to "The Veil" to expand their understanding of the Cosmos and their place in it. 

The least biologically like their human origins. Although also partially techno-bio race they are 90% biological and only 10% technological. They have altered their DNA to get energy from light via photosynthesis. Their technology is largely organic in nature with minimal traditional technology. Most of their traditional technologies are for maintaining their connection to "The Veil". From their buildings, infrastructure, to their Starships, most of their civilization is in one way or another biologically alive (but partly technological) and are "grown" not "built" and are all directly connected to "The Veil". 

Their is no central government, the aggregate needs of the civilization are fully understood by their decentralized neural-net (some what akin to how block chain systems worked in the early 21st century). Anything that any one person in the Neo-Ionians Collective needs can be grown (provided their are the needed resources). Their Anarchist Collective Society has no traditional economic system, making their use of resources hyper efficient, but their efficiency comes at the price of growing ("building") their civilization takes longer than the other two civilizations. 

This is balanced by any new star system they expand into they start with every single technology fully ready to go. They can grow any building, in any order, and have access to growing any unit, be it star ships or ground units. They also cannot be cut off from their civilization. None of their star systems will "break away". 

Expansion speed: Slow 

Strengths: Maximum resource efficiency. Full tech tree when expanding into a new star system. No break away star systems. Can absorb break away Neo-Puritans with propaganda, or diplomacy (but not via military force). They can hold out longer in a long attrition War better than the other two races. Perfectly stable "economy". Due to the decentralized nature of their knowledge, any unit can rebuild the entire civilization, requiring 100% of all units and buildings for that player to be out of the game. 

Weakness: Slow (but efficient) use of resources. Takes longer to gather resources and grow ("build") building, infrastructure, starships and ground units than the two races 

Break systems: None, but they can absorb break away Neo-Puritans star systems into their civilization with propaganda, or diplomacy 



NEO-PURITANS

The most fully classically "human" of the three races. Their culture, government, military, economy, societal norms, religions, and institutions are tied deeply to Cold War-era Western-NATO ideology. 

“Most Terrifying Words – 'I'm from the government and I'm here to help.” US President Ronald Reagan, Cerca 1986 A.D.

Expansion speed: Medium 

Strengths: Very fast economic action; mining resources, spending resources, building factories, building units. They can "recover" Star Systems that get cut off from the home world government by military force, propaganda, or diplomacy. 

Weakness: Very inefficient economy; Neo-Puritans only can get 1/3 the use of mining resources as the Neo-Ionians who are 100% efficient at mining. Star Systems will "break away" if cut off from the central government, or propaganda from the Neo-Ionians or the Neo-Pythagoreans 

Break away star system: If a star system gets cut off from the central government it becomes an NPC "player" controlled by the computer that doesn't try to expand, but hunkers down and digs in until taken over by force, propaganda, or diplomacy. 




NEO-PYHAGREANS

Neo-Pythagoreans have fully embraced technology and are effectively 80% technology and only 20% biological. 

"...the only thing worse than slavery is the illusion of freedom" ~ Unknown, Cerca 2016 A.D. 
“Knowledge is valuable, but ignorance is priceless” ~ Unknown Cerca 2018 A.D.

Their Government is built around control of knowledge and the minds are tied to a central neural-net that's governed at the top by a closed source Master Control System human-AI hybrid hyper genius level intelligence who is the only "person" with full free will. From there, with lesser free will, are the Node Lords. After the Node Lords are the Local Regulators that govern over the Drones of society. For the Drones, all they know comes from above them and they have no access to outside information of any kind, although they still have some level of "free will" how much free will does one have if all knowledge you have fully controlled from above, so for the Drones everything they are told from above is absolute truth, and they are "free" of all worries about what to think is or isn't true and they are "free" to just enjoy life following the will of the State (Master Control System). 

They are directly connected to "The Veil" and use to keep order and control in a technocratic dystopian super state. 

Expansion speed: Fast 

Strengths: Immune to propaganda. Fastest economic action; mining resources, spending resources, building factories, building units, but only as the Empire is smaller (as the Empire grows more resources are needed at the top-to-down to manage to "system" to control everything). Economy starts at 90% of mining efficiency. Star System tech tree buildings and infrastructures are built faster than the other two races 

Weakness: Progressively inefficient economy as the Empire expands (more energy to manage control of knowledge). If a Star System gets cut off from the neural-net, it becomes an expanding menace "clone" of the civilization of The Neo-Pythagoreans that cannot be recovered that's a threat to all three civilizations. Node Lords and Local Regulators can be "targeted" to break off entire parts of the Empire. The economic inefficiencies of the Neo-Pythagoreans follow an inverse logarithmic slope capping at being 90% inefficient (from 90%+ to 90%-) 

Break away systems: Neo-Pythagoreans that get off from the Master Control System will be an computer controlled player that's a menace for all other players
